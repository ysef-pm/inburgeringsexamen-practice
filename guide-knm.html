The KNM exam: what it covers and how to practise

Updated September 2026 — reflects the KNM update of 1 July 2025

KNM — Kennis van de Nederlandse Maatschappij — isn't a language test. It checks whether you know how everyday life in the Netherlands works: who to call, what's expected, what your rights are. Most people pass it once they practise the type of question, because the questions are about situations, not trivia.

The format at a glance

WhatComputer-based multiple-choice exam at a DUO location
WhoRequired under both Wi2013 (A2 route) and Wi2021 (B1 route)
Time45 minutes
Cost€50 per attempt
ResultUsually within eight weeks, in Mijn Inburgering

You'll see "40 questions, pass with 26" (or 28) quoted online. Those numbers come from private course providers and don't agree with each other — DUO doesn't publish them. Don't aim for a cut-off; aim to be solid on all eight themes.

The eight KNM themes

  1. Work and income — contracts, payslips, UWV, looking for work, benefits, taxes.
  2. Manners, norms and values — punctuality, equality, directness, how neighbours interact.
  3. Housing — renting, huurtoeslag, the landlord's and tenant's responsibilities, waste rules.
  4. Health and healthcare — the huisarts as the first step, insurance, 112 vs. the huisartsenpost.
  5. History and geography — key periods, the provinces, water management.
  6. Institutions — the gemeente, DUO, IND, Belastingdienst: who does what.
  7. Constitution and rule of law — fundamental rights, elections, the police and courts.
  8. Education and upbringing — compulsory schooling, the school system, child benefits, parenting norms.

The July 2025 update added extra attention to topics such as self-determination (including for women) and knowledge of the Holocaust — practice material written before mid-2025 may miss these.

How the questions work

Your child has a high fever on Saturday night. Your huisarts is closed. What do you do?

A. Call 112
B. Call the huisartsenpost
C. Wait until Monday and call the huisarts

Answer: B. 112 is for life-threatening emergencies; the huisartsenpost handles urgent care outside office hours.

Most questions look like this: an everyday situation, three plausible options. The trap is usually an answer that would be normal in another country. Understanding why the Dutch answer is what it is makes the next ten questions easier.

How to practise KNM

  • Practise by theme, so you notice which areas you're weak in — most people have one or two.
  • Review every wrong answer. Read the explanation, not just the correct letter.
  • Do full timed rounds in the last week so 45 minutes feels comfortable.
  • Use current material — check it covers the 2025 themes.
